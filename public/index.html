Here's what's happening.

  1. Plan your walk. Set your time, shade, rest-stop, and rain preferences. Local Llama can interpret a written request.
  2. Compare three routes. Each card shows its best matching departure, predicted building and tree shade, weather, and bench. Select a card to highlight it on the map.
  3. Explore, then save. The slider previews shade at an instant. Your route statistics follow the sun throughout the whole walk. Save a card to carry outside.

What is real, and what is simulated?

Sun position is calculated from the date, time, and Colombo coordinates. Buildings, benches, routes, and forecasts are synthetic. This is a working simulation, not a real-street recommendation.

What does the match score mean?

Preference fit, not safety or accuracy. Route fit weights building and tree shade 60%, shorter uninterrupted exposure 25%, and rest-stop fit 15%. Rest priority uses 45/20/35. Without a requested bench, the remaining weights are rescaled. Overall match is 75% route fit and 25% weather fit.

Weather fit weights feels-like preference 50%, dryness 35%, and gust preference 15%. The default feels-like band is 22–30°C; fit falls linearly over 12°C outside it. Rain fit uses maximum hourly rain probability and amount relative to 5 mm; gust fit falls between 15 and 50 km/h. Skip-rain excludes ≥60% probability or ≥1 mm in any overlapping hour. Storm windows are excluded.

Bench fit uses shade on arrival (60 points) and closeness to halfway (40 points). Unknown geometry stays unknown; substantial gaps suppress scores. Walking time excludes pauses. Tree shade uses synthetic opaque circular canopies at known heights. Real foliage can let light through. Terrain, accessibility, bench occupancy, and UV protection aren't modelled.

No external maps, telemetry, or remote inference in the default local setup. Solar calculations use SunCalc.
